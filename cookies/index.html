Cookies Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

TechWokx ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on techwokx.online to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site performance, and deliver personalized content. This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They contain information about your browsing activity and preferences. Cookies serve several purposes:

  • Remembering login information and preferences
  • Understanding how visitors use our website
  • Personalizing your experience
  • Measuring the effectiveness of marketing campaigns
  • Improving website functionality and security

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic features such as page navigation and access to secure areas. Without these cookies, the website cannot operate correctly.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
session_id Maintains user session during browsing Session

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. They collect anonymous data about pages visited, time spent on pages, and user journeys. This information helps us improve website performance and user experience.

These cookies remember your choices and preferences to provide a personalized experience. They may store information about your language preferences, theme selections, or other customizations.

These cookies are used to track your browsing habits and deliver personalized advertisements. We use Meta Pixel and other advertising platforms to measure campaign performance and reach you with relevant content across the web.

2.5 Third-Party Cookies

Third-party cookies are set by domains other than the one you're visiting. We allow trusted third parties such as Google, Meta, and analytics providers to set cookies on our site to deliver their services. You can control these through your browser settings.

3. How We Use Cookie Data

  • Session Management: Keeping you logged in and maintaining your browsing session
  • Analytics: Understanding visitor behavior and website traffic patterns
  • Personalization: Customizing your experience based on your preferences
  • Marketing: Measuring ad campaign effectiveness and delivering targeted content
  • Security: Preventing fraud and protecting against unauthorized access
  • Performance: Optimizing website speed and functionality

4. Your Cookie Choices

4.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Accept or reject all cookies
  • Accept only essential cookies
  • Receive a notification when a cookie is being set
  • Delete cookies from your device

Instructions for popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome Cookie Settings
  • Firefox Cookie Settings
  • Safari Cookie Settings
  • Microsoft Edge Cookie Settings

4.2 Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. While we respect DNT signals, we cannot guarantee that third-party services will honor them. Please check your browser's documentation for enabling DNT.

4.3 Opt-Out Options

You can opt out of specific tracking services:

  • Google Analytics: Download and install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Meta Pixel: Adjust your ad preferences in your Meta account settings

5. Cookie Consent

When you first visit techwokx.online, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You can choose to:

  • Accept All: Allow all cookies, including marketing and analytics cookies
  • Reject All: Decline all non-essential cookies
  • Customize: Select which types of cookies you wish to accept

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by updating your settings or clearing your browser cookies.

6. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable essential cookies, some features of techwokx.online may not work properly. Specifically:

  • You may not be able to log in to your account
  • Your preferences may not be saved
  • Some website features may be unavailable

Disabling marketing and analytics cookies will not affect website functionality but may impact the relevance of content and advertisements you receive.

7. Cookie Duration

Cookies have different lifespans:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device until they expire or you delete them

Refer to the cookie tables above for specific duration information for each cookie we use.

8. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect cookie data, including encryption and secure transmission protocols (HTTPS). However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Considerations

Depending on your location, different data protection laws may apply. If you are located in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requires explicit consent before non-essential cookies are set. We comply with all applicable international data protection regulations.
